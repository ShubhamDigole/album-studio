# Design library licences

Everything in `assets/library/` may be used commercially (in printed and online albums) without attribution.

## Original work

These were drawn or generated for this project and carry no third-party rights:

- `patterns/*.svg` (18 seamless tiles, each with `--gold`, `--ivory`, `--ink` and `--rose` colourways)
- `art/*.svg` (103 ornaments, frames, motifs and illustrations)
- `designs/*.jpg` and the `designs` in library.json (thumbnails rendered with neutral, generated placeholder pictures)
- `backgrounds/` watercolour-blush, watercolour-lilac, watercolour-sage, watercolour-sky, watercolour-peach, bokeh-gold, bokeh-rose, fairy-lights, gradient-dusk, gradient-midnight, gradient-mint (generated watercolour, bokeh, light and gradient grounds)
